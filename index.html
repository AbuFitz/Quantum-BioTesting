Before you book.

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can book directly online without a GP referral. Choose your preferred clinic location and appointment date, and we will handle the rest.

How should I prepare?

Fast for 8–12 hours beforehand (water is fine), stay well hydrated, continue prescription medication as normal and avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours. Wear a short-sleeved or loose-fitting top. Read the full guide.

How long will results take?

Standard results arrive in 2–5 working days from sample receipt at the laboratory (4 working days for the Women’s Health Check). An express service can return results in as little as 2 hours; please ask our team about availability when booking.

Can I share my results with my GP?

Yes. Your digital report is formatted to a clinical standard and can be shared with your GP or any other healthcare provider.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

Our team confirms your appointment details by email. An enquiry alone does not confirm a booking, and payment is due on confirmation. Clinics are available across the UK; available locations are shown as you book.
