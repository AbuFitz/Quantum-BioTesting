02 Why comprehensive

Why routine tests aren’t enough.

Standard NHS blood tests typically screen for a handful of basic markers. That is enough to flag obvious illness, but not enough to give a complete picture of your metabolic health, hormonal balance, nutritional status or early disease risk.

Our health checks analyse 300+ biomarkers for men and up to 350 for women, giving you and your doctor a far fuller view from a single blood test.

Every result is reviewed by a clinical specialist. Your report includes plain-English commentary explaining what each reading means and whether any further attention is warranted.

05 Preparation

Simple steps for accurate results.

Following these guidelines helps your results be as accurate and clinically meaningful as possible.

  1. 1

    Fast for 8–12 hours

    Fast for at least 8 hours, ideally 10–12, before your appointment. Water is permitted and encouraged. A morning appointment after an overnight fast is simplest for most people.

  2. 2

    Stay well hydrated

    Drink plenty of water in the 24 hours before and on the morning of your test. Good hydration makes the draw more comfortable and improves the accuracy of certain markers.

  3. 3

    Continue all medications

    Take prescribed medication as normal unless your doctor has advised otherwise. Where possible, take vitamin and mineral supplements after your blood draw.

  4. 4

    Avoid strenuous exercise

    Avoid intense exercise for 24 hours beforehand. Heavy exercise can temporarily raise inflammatory markers, creatine kinase and certain enzymes.

Specific medical requirements? If you take regular medication, have a health condition or are unsure about preparation, please contact our team before your appointment.

09 Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can book directly online without a GP referral. Choose your preferred clinic location and appointment date, and we will handle the rest.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Fast for 8–12 hours beforehand (water is fine), stay well hydrated, continue prescription medication as normal and avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours. Wear a short-sleeved or loose-fitting top for easy access to your arm. Full guidance is in the preparation section.

How long will my results take?

Standard results are delivered in 2–5 working days from sample receipt at the laboratory (4 working days for the Women’s Health Check). An express service can return results in as little as 2 hours. Please ask our team about availability when booking.

Can I share my results with my GP?

Yes. Your digital report is formatted to a clinical standard and can be shared with your GP or any other healthcare provider. Many clients use their results to start more informed conversations with their doctor.

Where are the clinics?

We have clinic locations across the United Kingdom, including sites in London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Liverpool, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Bristol, Belfast, Sheffield, Newcastle and High Wycombe. Available locations are shown during the booking process.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

Our team confirms your appointment details by email. An enquiry alone does not confirm a booking, and payment is due on confirmation. See our Terms & Conditions for details.
