  • “Client” means the individual purchasing and/or receiving the Service.
  • “Results” means the laboratory report and clinically reviewed results document provided to the Client.

2. Eligibility

The Service is available only to:

  • Individuals aged 18 years or older.
  • Individuals who are current residents of the United Kingdom.
  • Individuals who are not currently an inpatient in a hospital, hospice, or residential care facility.

By purchasing the Service, the Client confirms they meet all eligibility criteria. The Company reserves the right to decline or cancel a booking where eligibility cannot be confirmed.

3. Nature of the service

Quantum BioTesting health checks are private health information services. They are not a substitute for NHS services and do not constitute:

  • A diagnosis of any medical condition
  • A recommendation to alter prescribed medication or treatment
  • An emergency or urgent care service

Any physician consultation included in the Service is for the purpose of reviewing and explaining test results only. It does not establish an ongoing patient-doctor relationship.

In the event of a medical emergency, the Client must contact 999 or attend their nearest Accident & Emergency department immediately.

4. Booking & contract formation

A contract between the Client and the Company is formed when:

  1. The Client submits a booking enquiry (via the website form or telephone), and
  2. The Company sends written confirmation of the appointment by email.

An enquiry submission alone does not constitute a confirmed booking. Payment is due upon confirmation as detailed in the confirmation email. The Company reserves the right to decline any booking at its discretion.

5. Payment

The all-inclusive price for each health check is £995. This price covers all elements of the Service described on our website and in these Terms. There are no additional charges.

Payment must be made in full prior to the appointment. We accept payment by major credit and debit cards. VAT is not applicable as the Service qualifies as an exempt medical supply under UK VAT law.

6. Cancellation & refund policy

The Client may cancel their appointment subject to the following conditions:

  • More than 7 calendar days before appointment: Full refund minus a £50 administrative fee.
  • 3–7 calendar days before appointment: 50% refund of the total amount paid.
  • Less than 48 hours before appointment: No refund is available, unless cancellation is due to a documented medical emergency.
  • No-show (failure to attend without notice): No refund.

Cancellations must be made in writing by email to bookings@quantumbt.co.uk or by telephone. The time and date of notice receipt by the Company determines which tier applies.

The Company may cancel or reschedule an appointment in exceptional circumstances (including clinic closure or physician unavailability). In such cases, a full refund will be offered or an alternative appointment arranged at the Client’s discretion.

7. Preparation requirements

Accurate results depend on the Client following the preparation guidelines provided at booking. If the Client fails to follow these guidelines, the Company will:

  • Proceed with the appointment at its discretion, noting any deviation in the report, or
  • Reschedule the appointment. A rescheduling fee of £75 may apply if the failure is attributable to the Client.

8. Results & limitations

The Company warrants that all laboratory analysis will be conducted in UKAS ISO 15189 accredited facilities using validated methods. However, the Client acknowledges that:

  • No diagnostic test is 100% accurate. Results reflect measurements at a single point in time.
  • Reference ranges provided are based on population statistics and may not apply to every individual.
  • A result within a “normal” range does not guarantee the absence of a medical condition.
  • Abnormal results should always be discussed with the Client’s own GP or treating physician before any clinical decision is made.

9. Physician consultation scope (Women’s Health Check)

The physician may explain clinical significance of individual results and recommend the Client share specific findings with their GP. The physician may not prescribe medication, issue NHS referrals, or provide formal diagnoses.
